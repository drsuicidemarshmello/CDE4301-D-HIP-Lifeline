# CDE4301 EIM-434 Team Lifeline: Interventional Oncology
## Introduction
This project is undertaken as part of the Duke-NUS Health Innovator Program (DHIP), which brings together individuals from different academic disciplines to identify unmet clinical needs and develop innovative healthcare solutions. Our multidisciplinary team comprises five fellows with complementary backgrounds: Abhay Menon and Phua Kai Jie (Clinical Fellows), Sharleen Hu and Kirby Lee (Engineering Fellows), and Michelle Tan (Business Fellow). This project is supported by our clinical mentors, Dr Too and Dr Heng, from the Department of Vascular and Interventional Radiology at Singapore General Hospital (SGH).
